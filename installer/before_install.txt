Easy Life Setup
===============

Thanks for installing Easy Life.

Easy Life lets you build and run "Plays" — simple lists of things
your PC should do: open apps, launch games, go to websites,
wait, switch OBS scenes, and more.

Your PC runs the Plays. Your iPhone can control them from anywhere.

To get started:
  1. Open Easy Life.
  2. Create a username and password.
  3. Build your first Play from the Dashboard.

For help, see the app's Settings or the project README.

This installer does not change your data. Your Plays, settings and
paired phones stay in your Easy Life data folder — uninstalling the
app never deletes that folder.

~ Easy Life
