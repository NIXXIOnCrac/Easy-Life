Easy Life is installed.

Your Plays, settings and paired phones are kept in your Easy Life data
folder, which the uninstaller never touches:

  C:\Users\<you>\AppData\Local\Easy Life\data

If you want the app to come up on its own, tick "Start Easy Life when
I log in" — or just run it whenever you want from the Start Menu or
the desktop shortcut.

To control the app from your iPhone, open Settings in Easy Life and
turn on "Use your phone from anywhere".
